# Disk Free — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

Disk Free is a free, open-source macOS app that shows how much free space your volumes have. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your Mac.**

## What Disk Free collects

Nothing. Disk Free collects no personal data, no analytics, no usage statistics and no crash reports. It has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

## Network access

Disk Free has no network access of its own. It never contacts any server and sends nothing anywhere. The only network-related thing it does is read the free space of network volumes (SMB/AFP shares) that you have already mounted in macOS, through the operating system's own file system calls.

## Data stored on your Mac

Volume history (recent free-space readings) and your settings are stored only in the app's sandbox container on your Mac. They are never uploaded, and you can remove them at any time by deleting the app and its container.

## Spoken alerts

Spoken warnings use the speech synthesis built into macOS and run entirely on your Mac. No text or audio is sent to any service.

## Children

Disk Free collects no data from anyone, including children.
